Consider a nonnegative tensor of the form

\[
\mathcal{T}=\sum_{r=1}^R a_{1}^{(r)}\otimes \dots \otimes a_{d}^{(r)},\quad a_{j}^{(r)}\in \mathbb{R}^{n_j}_+ \setminus \{0\},\quad j\in [d].
\]

When can its latent components be identified? I study identifiability via positive scattering {cite:tensor-identifiability}.
