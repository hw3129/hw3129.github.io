For a nonnegative tensor \(R(f)=\mathbb{E}[\ell(f(X),Y)]\) When can their latent components be identified,

\[
\mathcal{T}=\sum_{r=1}^R a_{1}^{(r)}\otimes \dots \otimes a_{d}^{(r)},\quad a_{j}^{(r)}\in \mathbb{R}^{n_j}_+ \setminus \{0\},\quad j\in [d].
\]

and how sensitively do those components depend on the observed tensor? I study identifiability through positive scattering {cite:tensor-identifiability} and, in a complementary project, local Lipschitz stability under the same structural condition {cite:tensor-stability}.
